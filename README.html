Final. Pack 2026-10-18-students-1. Set your machine up with Before TD 1.

Kit of 14 October 2026 for the three sessions of 21 October, 18 November and 11 December. The database and the twenty questions are the same for every sub-group. Read the environment guide first. Preparation was due by 18 October; the environment is checked when TD 1 opens at 13:30. Each assignment gives the schedule of its afternoon.

Start

cd kit-td1
python3 check_access.py                       # Python, database hash, one probe per authorised model
python3 skeleton-multi-llm-en.py --demo       # the whole chain on three simulated models, no key, no network
python3 atelier-routage.py                    # the seven routing scenarios, no network

Then step 1 of enonce-td1-en: fill in reference-td1.csv. Step 2 uses the allocation and --models / --cases commands in the assignment. The base batch is 100 cells (60 Groq, 40 OpenRouter); controls and retries are separate, with at most one retry per cell. Each operator checks their own allowance. Keep the CSV and JSONL attempts.

The assessment sheet, the hand-in page and the hand-in templates are one folder above this runnable kit. Before TD 1 gives the preparation steps to follow before the first session.
